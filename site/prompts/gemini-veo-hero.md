# Bimax hero film

Create an 8-second cinematic product film, landscape 16:9, 4K, 24 fps, one continuous shot with no cuts.

A warm ember-orange instruction card moves into a precise spatial system made from five deep graphite rectangular code frames. The camera begins outside the structure at a three-quarter angle, then performs a slow controlled dolly forward through the layered frames. As the card travels inward, four small matte graphite code modules move from scattered positions into an ordered architecture around it. Near the end, the camera eases into a perfectly frontal view. A restrained teal verification frame closes around the system and a small dimensional teal checkmark locks into place above the orange card.

The metaphor is a human request entering a real software project, becoming an implemented change, and exiting verified.

Art direction: premium industrial design film, dark near-black studio environment, graphite metal with soft roughness, ember orange ceramic-metal card, restrained teal anodized metal, physically plausible reflections, subtle volumetric light, high material detail, crisp silhouette, cinematic depth, elegant and technical rather than futuristic or cyberpunk. Keep the object concentrated in the center-right and preserve clean dark negative space on the left for website typography.

Motion direction: steady camera, slow deliberate acceleration and deceleration, stable geometry, consistent object identity, no morphing, no spinning for decoration, no handheld movement. Every movement must explain input, assembly, or verification.

Do not include text, letters, logos, people, hands, screens, terminals, fake user interfaces, particles, holograms, neon grids, lens dirt, glitches, or scene cuts. Avoid flicker, warped rectangles, duplicated modules, unstable edges, and sudden lighting changes.

The first frame should show the orange request card approaching scattered project layers. The last frame should show the complete verified system front-on, suitable for holding as a final scroll state.
